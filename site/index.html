    The designer

    Anthony Demonia

    The Bull and Bloom is the studio of floral designer Anthony Demonia, based in Upstate New York. The name comes from the drawing on the card: something big and steady, stopped in its tracks by something small and beautiful. That's the feeling we aim for in every arrangement.

    Anthony works with what's in season, sources locally where he can, and keeps the palette honest: no dyed stems, no filler for the sake of filling.
