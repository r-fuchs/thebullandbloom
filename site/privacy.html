Privacy

What we collect

When you place an order, we collect your name, email, phone number, and — if you're having your bouquet delivered — your delivery address, along with the order details (size, day, and any note or card message you leave for Anthony). We only collect this when you place an order; just browsing the site tells us nothing about you.

Payment

Payment is handled entirely by Stripe. Your card number never reaches our systems — we never see it and never store it. Stripe has its own privacy policy covering payment data: stripe.com/privacy.

How we use it

We use your information to prepare and deliver your order, to email your confirmation and any updates, and to put your order on the studio's calendar so Anthony knows what to make and when. Our order calendar and email run through the studio's own Google account. Google does not get your information for advertising — it's simply how Anthony keeps his day organized and sends mail.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share your information with anyone. There are no advertising trackers on this site. We won't send you marketing email unless you ask us to.

Where it lives and for how long

The site and your order data are hosted on Cloudflare. We keep order records for as long as we need them for bookkeeping and tax purposes, and no longer.

Your choices

Want to know what we hold about you, or want it removed? Email thebullandbloom@gmail.com and we'll take care of it.
